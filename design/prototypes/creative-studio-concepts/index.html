AXIOM Creative Studio · three concepts for review

Three ways to make the same tile

Each concept is a working prototype of the Studio built around one brief, one client and the same words, so you can judge the shape of the work rather than the content. They share the Studio's own colours and type on purpose: the differences are in navigation, layout, where the tools sit, how the canvas is used and how decisions get made. None of this touches the production app.

Client

Harbourline Ferries, a synthetic demo client. Campaign: Harbour crossings.

Brief

Two more morning crossings. Tell weekday riders the 6.40 and 7.10 from Pier 2 start Monday 9 March.

Copy

"Two more morning crossings from Monday." The 6.40 and 7.10 now leave Pier 2 for Northbridge Wharf, weekdays. Plan your crossing.

Concept A · direct control

Production Desk

A stable, tool-rich desk for people who know what they want to change. Everything is on screen at once and stays put.

  • Open the Instagram story: its supporting line fails the safe-area check at the foot.
  • Select it in Layers, set Y to 72 in the inspector, and watch the check pass and Approve design unlock.
  • Drag words on the canvas, nudge with the arrow keys, undo with Ctrl+Z.

Concept B · exploration and choice

Creative Direction Board

A pin-up wall for choosing a creative route before anything is produced. No canvas, no inspector: boards, reasons and decisions.

  • Read the Partner's recommendation, then "Why not the others".
  • Shortlist the departure board, Compare, then Combine two: take the photograph from one and the layout from the other.
  • Set aside "Twenty more minutes" with a reason, open a board, and Continue into production.
Open Concept B
Concept C · chosen · guided, end to end

Guided Production

Seven steps from brief to delivery in one focused column. At Design the page opens into a full-window editor laid out like your reference: a tool rail with an Add item panel, the canvas, and a Style panel with exact spacing, size, overflow, typography and a colour picker that shows contrast.

  • Read the brief: the wait card shows the call, elapsed time, typical time and an estimated share. Carry on while it runs.
  • Mark each piece's copy ready, then open the editor: click the headline, set its distance to the edges in pixels, open Color and watch the contrast ratio change.
  • Switch pieces in the top bar, add a button from Add item, open the Creative Partner from the rail, generate the photograph and keep editing.

Chosen: C

C is the direction, with its editor laid out like your reference. This prototype is the visual spec for the production Studio, which is being changed to match.

One question for C. The Studio today never shows a percentage worked out from the clock, because it can mislead. C shows one, labelled "estimated" and based on how long that step usually takes, next to the elapsed and typical times. If you choose C, say whether production should do the same.

Where the files are

In the repository at design/prototypes/creative-studio-concepts/, outside docs/, so GitHub Pages never serves them and the app never loads them. To open them on a Mac:

cd ~/Axiom/design/prototypes/creative-studio-concepts
python3 -m http.server 8080
# then open http://localhost:8080/concept-a.html (b, c)
  • Nothing is sent anywhere: no worker, no model, no key.
  • The client, brief, figures and references are invented.
